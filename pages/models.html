Current Position

The model landscape is confirmed and strengthened by new evidence. Qwen3.6-27B Dense with MTP remains the quality ceiling at 77.2% SWE-bench Verified, now with confirmed 100-142 tok/s on RTX 4090 via native MTP in llama.cpp. The Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MoE story gets even stronger: confirmed running on just 12GB VRAM with MTP flags (--spec-type mtp --spec-draft-n-max 2), achieving 80-98% token acceptance rates. On RTX 4070, it hits 65-75 tok/s vs 51 tok/s baseline — a genuine 30-47% MTP uplift. Qwen3-30B-A3B (the non-3.6 version) is now community consensus 'sweet spot' for local self-hosting, ranking top-10 on coding/reasoning leaderboards among locally-runnable models. This creates a clear model ladder: Qwen3.6-27B Dense (best quality, needs 16-20GB) → Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MoE (best efficiency, 6-12GB) → Qwen3-30B-A3B (proven workhorse, 6-10GB) → Llama-3.3-70B (needs 40-70GB, outclassed for coding). Notably, the Strix Halo benchmarks showing Qwen3-Coder 30B BF16 at 9.19 tok/s on 128GB shared memory demonstrate that MoE models are the correct architecture choice for bandwidth-limited platforms — confirming the previous analysis.
