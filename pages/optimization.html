🔬 Optimization Analysis

Last check: 2026-05-26 09:17:34

Current Position

The optimization landscape has consolidated around three proven stacks, each with strengthened evidence. llama.cpp MTP for Qwen3.6 models is now confirmed in mainline with specific flags (--spec-type mtp --spec-draft-n-max 2), delivering 30-47% speedup on RTX 4070 and 2-2.5x on RTX 4090. The ik_llama fork offers alternative MTP with additional quantization options — the gap is closing but mainline llama.cpp remains the safer bet. Ollama 0.19 MLX is now the default backend for Apple Silicon, not an opt-in feature. Confirmed benchmarks: 134 tok/s on Llama 4 8B Q4, 72 tok/s on Qwen 3.5 14B Q4, ~20 tok/s on Llama 70B Q4 — all on M4 Max. The addition of NVFP4 quantization format support in Ollama 0.19 is notable, bringing cloud-grade quantization to local setups. For Strix Halo specifically, Vulkan is confirmed significantly faster than ROCm/HIP — this is a critical finding for Strix Halo buyers. The community consensus on serving backends has solidified: Ollama for single-user, vLLM/ExLlamaV2 for multi-user production, llama.cpp for bare-metal tuning. Unsloth + QLoRA established as the fine-tuning standard (LoRA rank 32-64, target q_proj/v_proj).
